# Seaside pier studies

These structure previews use `pier.New` for the decks, pilings and rails
and `pier.NewWheel` for the Santa Monica and Scheveningen wheels. All
four go through `cove.New` for shoreline and water staging. Beaches,
pavilions and most secondary dimensions are authored approximations.

- [Oceanside Municipal Pier][oceanside] uses the city's 1,954 ft public
  length. The end pavilion evokes the pier before the 2024 fire.
- [Santa Monica Pier][santa] uses the city's approximate 2,135 ft extent.
  [Pacific Park][pacific] supplies the 85 ft wheel and its 20 gondolas.
- [Brighton Palace Pier][brighton] uses the operator's 1,722 ft length.
- [Scheveningen][scheveningen] uses the 400 m gross extent and two decks.
  The [SkyView operator][skyview] gives 36 gondolas and 50 m above water.

The architectural review uses the shared calm infinite plane so the
piers and their silhouettes are readable from end to end.
