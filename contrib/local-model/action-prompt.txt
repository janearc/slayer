Output one JSON object with width, height, rays, camera and aim. Width and height are pixels. Rays is the number of rays along each side of a pixel.
For camera and aim:
- Leave it alone: {"mode":"keep"}.
- Set an absolute point: {"mode":"set","xyz":[X,Y,Z]}.
- Move from the current point: {"mode":"move","by":{"east":2,"up":3}}.
- Aim at the ball centre: {"mode":"ball","by":{}}. Aim near the ball centre: use "ball" and name the directions and distances in "by".
Use only the directions explicitly requested. Direction keys are east, west, north, south, up and down. All distances are positive metres. Higher and above mean up; lower and below mean down. Include every requested direction. The fixture does the coordinate arithmetic.
