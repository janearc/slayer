Maunakea campus study, 2026-11-15

Terrain is the retained NOAA Hawaii CRM2023 native-grid Big Island subset,
SHA256 37c3cf3b48849d599950f255e64fed2bed7eb744ef18bf73d2375e9f0fc0f830.
Its WGS84 grid is one arcsecond; heights are MSL, treated as radial offsets
on the existing authored sphere. This does not resolve roads or foundations.

Nine optical/submillimeter building positions use IfA's September25,1996
aerial survey, published by NASA IRTF:
https://irtfweb.ifa.hawaii.edu/observing/telescopeCoordinates.php
https://legacy.ifa.hawaii.edu/mko-coordinates/coordinates.shtml
The latitude/longitude columns are NAD83. For this coarse scenery preview
they are treated as WGS84 without an epoch/datum transformation. The scene
uses DEM heights, not that table's instrument elevations in feet.
East/south offsets use a spherical small-angle conversion about
19:49:34N,155:28:28W. Coordinates are scenery placement, not a new survey.

VLBA position uses NRAO's WGS84 station list:
https://science.nrao.edu/facilities/vlba/docs/manuals/oss2023B/sites
19:48:04.97N,155:27:19.81W. Its quoted ellipsoid elevation is NOT mixed
with NOAA MSL heights. The station is downslope and has a separate wide view.

The SMA centre is approximately placed from the official summit map.
Its eight-dish compact arrangement is authored, not surveyed pad locations
or a claimed observing configuration:
https://hilo.hawaii.edu/maunakea/maps/
https://hilo.hawaii.edu/maunakea/documents/Maunakea-summit-map.pdf
The map is historical and is not the current inventory.

Inventory: KeckI/II, Subaru, IRTF, CFHT, GeminiNorth, UH88, UKIRT,
JCMT, SMA and VLBA. UKIRT remains a building; UH announced science closure
for September15,2026. CSO and HokuKea are omitted after their 2024 removal:
https://www.hawaii.edu/news/2026/07/02/uh-continues-ukirt-decommissioning/
https://www.hawaii.edu/news/2024/07/02/second-observatory-removed-maunakea/

Building geometry, orientation, lighting, camera and ground colour are
authored approximations. The Kecks share the same static pointing.
No connecting service buildings, roads, survey-grade footprints, operating
status telemetry or mechanically articulated tracking are represented.
The other factory families have less detail than the revised Keck/Subaru.
Sources checked through their primary web pages; no new shelf pin claimed.

Heard F05 precinct revision: the sourced positions above now also place
Subaru, IRTF, CFHT, Gemini North, UH88, UKIRT, JCMT and SMA around the Kecks.
The local service buildings, mechanical plant, ladder and vent detail,
connecting access-road paths and graded pads are authored approximations
from the supplied UH summit map and photos. They are not survey geometry.
Patchy snow and pale volcanic weathering are authored reference weather;
vegetation is confined to lower altitude cover. CSO and HokuKea remain omitted.
