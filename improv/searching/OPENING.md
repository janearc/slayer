# heard. - opening direction for screenplay v3

This opening direction belongs to [screenplay v3](SHOOT-v3.md) and its
[arrangement](MUSIC-v3.md). They replaced the initial ten-bar proposal
and the 64-bar allocation. See the [film guide](README.md) for the v6 take.

## First encounters

Bars 1-2: measured soundtrack spectrograms in darkness. The small
heard. title enters at 2.3, after listening is already established.
The display uses the new mix and retains its analysis settings.

Bars 3-4: both Kecks together above Cloudsea, shared slew and settle.
Fixed camera first, so motion of optics is distinct from photography.
Fixed foundations and service yard, warm layered mist, dark foreground.

Bar 5: early Rubin. Angular enclosure, service wing, Chilean ridge.
A small metal tick changes the musical texture. No default ELT beam rig.

Bars 6-7: inside Subaru, near the floor. Warm mirrors and trusses above,
enclosure ribs and floor stable, open sky through the aperture. Separate
the instrument's settle from the camera's finishing upward movement.

Bars 8-9: inside ELT, with its scale legible. Follow the launcher from
inside to the narrow amber beam outside; structure occludes it correctly.
An enclosed interior is required, not a cutaway catalogue specimen.

Bars 10-11: behind both Kecks into the Milky Way. They abandon one field
and settle on a second together. One sky clock; independently moving mist.

Bar 12: Griffith through Los Angeles haze, held and familiar.
Bar 13: the Sun visibly sets behind Chilean terrain.
Bar 14: a later Hawaiian dawn, visibly crossing the ridge. No rescue.

Bars 15-16: varied decisions, interior Rubin, rear Subaru, paired Kecks.
Bar 17: a final correction passes across the instruments.
Bars 18-19: shared lock and hold. Weather moves; searching has stopped.

## What has to connect

The improved manufacture models exist but world.go still uses the old
generic optical builder. A shot must select the actual current family
and its complete set. New catalogue studies alone do not close this gap.

Four groups must stay separate: foundations and service buildings;
enclosure/shutters; azimuth mount; altitude optics and attached launchers.
Both Kecks use one track. Instruments slew in elevation as well as azimuth.

Use the approved Cloudsea terrain, forest, rocks, yard and layered mist.
The selected spooky summit low view defines dark silhouettes, warm depth
and sharp amber beams. Carry the visual relationships, not exposure
numbers copied between different rendering pipelines.

Stars and Milky Way rotate coherently. Sun, Moon and illumination share
astronomical time within a continuous shot. Cuts advance between nights;
do not squeeze repeated lunar arcs into the same shot. The sunset and
sunrise must be deliberately framed.
