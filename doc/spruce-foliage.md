# The old spruces, with physical shoots

The named factory entries `spruce` and `norway` now use a draft physical
foliage wrapper. It retains their seeded whorled scaffolds, including
Norway's hanging branchlets, and replaces terminal foliage sleeves with
shared recursive shoots. All execution checks wait for the review break.

Norway's unmarked major-branch sleeves are removed rather than needled.
Those branches show their retained bark; the hanging terminal branchlets
carry the physical shoots. Generic spruce marks its major foliage carriers
explicitly because it has no separate hanging branchlet layer.

The old construction rows and legacy conifer reference APIs remain in
place. The named factory's appearance deliberately changes. Existing
captures remain historical references; no new signatures or images have
been generated by this code-only update.

## What the needles represent

[Oregon State University's Norway spruce account](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/picea-abies)
describes needles around the twig and their four-sided form, together
with the hanging secondary-branch habit. We use that qualitative form,
without promoting its numerical dimensions into this recipe.

Spruce needle lengths, widths, thickness, spacing, rank count and angle
are picked in `manufacture/needled-shoot.yml`. Four authored radial ranks
alternate a half-rank phase along the twig. That is a construction rule,
not a measured phyllotaxis model. Generic spruce shares this treatment.

The recursion follows the same ABOP/Weber-Penn adaptation used for
[redwood and sequoia shoots](conifer-foliage.md). Needle dimensions remain
physical: a larger branch receives more fixed-size shoot cells instead
of stretching its needles.

## The detail ladder

`hull` uses a trunk and crown proxy. `clumps` retains the recursive woody
shoots with one closed foliage proxy per cell. `quick` draws closed
needle tetrahedra; `demo` uses the finer closed needle surfaces. Their
radius/width proportions give the spruce blades a three-dimensional form.

Eight coarse wood vertices retain the four ranks at both alternating
phases. Fine wood keeps those attachment vertices. The seeded major
limbs, shoot placement and reserved physical envelope are shared across
representations.

This uses ordinary mesh groups on the existing CPU/GPU path. It adds no
kernel primitive. CPU/native agreement, closed geometry, memory cost and
moving-view appearance remain unverified; the old conifer checks do not
validate this new named-factory geometry.

The 0.4 m spruce prototype has an estimated 7.8 m of recursive twig. At 4 mm
spacing and four ranks that is roughly 7,800 needles: about 31,000 quick faces
or 63,000 demo faces per prototype. This is a construction estimate, not an
observed allocation or timing.

Five length bins, two variants and three shoot details can retain thirty
prototypes; the later memory gate must measure that storage and the instances
separately.
