Water sampling: comparison with a 64-per-side finite reference

The same 32 by 32 patch at left 1160, top 1400 was drawn at 64 rays per
side (4096 samples per pixel) using the recorded executable and set. Their
SHA-256 hashes were rechecked and still match recipe.txt. The draw used the
software GPU, fixed exposure, SLAYER_SCROOGE=1 and fresh frame output.
Existing caches were used; the full transitive source closure is not proven.

This compares the existing raw 1, 2, 4, 16 and 32 per-side frames with the
new 64 per-side frame, using the same 0.345 isolated-positive luminance
threshold as report.txt. That threshold was chosen from the earlier 32-side
reference, not a defect cutoff. The original 32-side comparison remains in
report.txt and scores.json. The 64-side raw scores are in scores-64.json.

For 32 minus 64, signed mean RGB differences were (0.127348, 0.091529,
0.018478). The 32- and 64-side frames differ in raw light; neither this
comparison nor the repeat of developed pixels establishes convergence or raw
repeatability. Scores and isolated counts are not monotonic across these
candidate settings. The inspected sheet is enlarged 6x with nearest-neighbour
pixels; bright glints remain visible in both high-sample images.

The 64-side frame's linear luminance median was 0.409432, 95th percentile
2.273950, and maximum 11.742105. The corresponding 32-side values are
0.342587, 3.451069, and 22.813562 (report.txt). These are observations from
individual finite draws; the threshold remains fixed for this comparison.
