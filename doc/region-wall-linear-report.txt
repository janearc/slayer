Wall sampling: raw linear-light comparison

A 32 by 32 patch at left 183, top 392 in a 2048 by 1820 street frame.
All draws used the same recorded street set, fixed exposure, street camera,
software GPU, SLAYER_SCROOGE=1 and fresh frame draws. The executable SHA-256
was 65ddcb20b7bfdac9400297b78339baa8692b06003aaf6d18ff607b52b0a6004e;
the set SHA-256 was
a5b906416963004ce02cd4ef5eebd38e78b170262a3397a4b147fce26bb365fd.
Existing scene caches were used. The complete transitive input closure and
cache stability have not been proven, so this is an observation of the
recorded runs, not a provenance guarantee.

The reference is 32 rays per side, or 1024 samples per pixel. Its linear
luminance median was 0.0330178, 95th percentile 0.0364306, and maximum
0.0411373. It is a finite reference, not proven converged. No raw-light
repeat variation was measured.

The isolated-positive-residual threshold is 0.00364 linear luminance units,
approximately 10% of this wall reference's 95th percentile. This is a chosen
measurement threshold, not a defect cutoff. A counted pixel exceeds it in
candidate-minus-reference luminance and none of its eight neighbours does;
border pixels are excluded. Thresholds differ between wall and water, so
isolated counts should not be compared directly between those patches.

The mean absolute difference falls across these sample counts, but the isolated
positive count does not. Signed channel means and per-channel absolute errors
are in scores.json. These measurements do not establish which image is best
or prove reference convergence. The 6x nearest-neighbour sheet is for visual
review; the wall becomes more even at higher sampling, though some colored
flecks remain. No water or lighting tuning was made.

Raw files: reference-32.pfm and candidate-1.pfm, candidate-2.pfm,
candidate-4.pfm and candidate-16.pfm. scores.json names their original local
study paths. scoring-helper.go.txt records the Puzzlemouth helper that read
them and used the project's regionjudge. The helper reported zero difference
when comparing a raw fixture to itself. The old full-frame crop uses an
unconfirmed build and GPU mode and is not the reference for these scores.
