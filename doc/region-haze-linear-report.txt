The reference is 32 rays per side (1024 samples per pixel). Its linear
luminance median was 0.1457253, 95th percentile 0.1588396, and maximum
0.1789689. This finite reference has not been shown to converge. Raw repeat
variation was not measured.

The isolated-positive-residual threshold is 0.0159 linear luminance units,
approximately 10% of reference p95. This is a measurement choice, not a
defect cutoff. A counted pixel exceeds it in candidate-minus-reference
luminance and none of its eight neighbours does; border pixels are excluded.
Thresholds differ across patches, so isolated counts are not directly
comparable across the water, wall and haze studies.
