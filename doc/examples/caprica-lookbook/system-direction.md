# Caprica system: authored direction

System direction, 2026-11-27:

- Caprica is an Earth-like planet in orbit around Fresno, the lava drop.
- Amber is a yellow gas giant uncomfortably close to the larger star.
- Azure is the blue ice giant. Twelve Hatzegopteryx should soar in its
  atmosphere under the light of the two distant suns: an artistic scene.
- Fresno has an atmosphere in the story because comets have inundated it
  for millions of years. The present set does not simulate that deposition.

The present look book shows Caprica and Fresno only. The separate Amber
studies are not yet placed in this same system, and do not establish its
orbit around the larger star. Azure has not yet been located in
the current system constructor. These are open implementation requirements,
not claims that the complete system has already been rendered.
