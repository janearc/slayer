with Slayer d7a0b207f, Azazel jobs 197–200. All four completed with black
frames: they are unsuccessful studies, not accepted demonstrations. The
job paths name the original `/private/tmp` inputs and output locations; to

choose new output paths. Appearance settings are declared made up. See the
queue logs for execution receipts. The reason for the black images is not

Jupiter recipe. With that single scene change, Azazel job 207 produced a

does not establish the underlying renderer defect. All four v4 views show
contrasting palettes, but visual inspection found smooth, smeared bands and
white storm spots without visible swirl; the close views do not meet the
intended visual standard. A matched CPU close view was submitted to examine
whether GPU texture sampling contributes. CPU job 215 shows more narrow
filaments, while the white storms remain soft ovals; this comparison supports
GPU texture sampling as a contributor to lost detail, but not the sole reason
the scene falls short. The v4 jobs record original absolute paths; choose
fresh outputs when reproducing. The v6 study places a larger dark storm
near the visible centre and removes the white street. CPU job 216 shows a
