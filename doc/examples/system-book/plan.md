# System look book: proposed coverage

The book should show twelve distinguishable views of one fictional system.
Candidate coverage, subject to full-size visual inspection:

1. The stellar pair and authored wind stream (`transfer`).
2. The companion and authored capture flow (`capture`).
3. Amber's cloud body and atmospheric crown (`jovian-crown`).
4. Amber's escaping-tail study (`jovian-tail`).
5. Azure from orbit (`azure-orbit`).
6. The Azure flock, from the separate local-origin study when ready.
7. Fresno's atmosphere and wake (`lavastream`).
8. Fresno from Caprica (`drop-from-caprica`).
9. Caprica's quarter-phase globe, from the established look-book render.
10. Caprica's night-side crescent, from the established look-book render.
11. Caprica's cloud deck, from the established look-book render.
12. Fresno's crust and seams, from the established look-book render.

These are proposals, not claims that the cameras show their intended subjects.
Candidate shots use a copy of `sets/caprica-binary/working-system.yaml` at
integration tip 02afa87e9; the preserved original draft and its revision are
not modified. The draft's Amber and Azure positions are fixed epoch artistic
placements, not propagated orbits. Wind, capture, Fresno haze and the crown
are authored wisps; they do not simulate accretion, atmosphere formation or
an emissive aurora. The comet fragments in the set are static placements,
not a simulated breakup. Azure's flock uses an artistic local-origin scene,
with two enlarged light cues, not an established global rebase. Scale and
lighting across the working scene remain unverified. Captions must describe
what is visible, and the visual researcher will inspect the candidates.
