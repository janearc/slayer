# A crowded summit

These are draft inputs for the quality-ceiling study, not render receipts.
The site has an ELT, Rubin, Subaru, both Kecks and SOLARC, with cranes,
trucks, platforms, crates and visitor telescopes in the clearing. Outside
it are 6,500 redwoods and 1,500 sequoias, all at `demo` detail.

That is the highest existing factory representation. The stand adapter
shares four seeded tree variants per species; these are 8,000 placements,
not 8,000 independently grown geometries. Terrain samples are four metres
apart. The layout is fictional and keeps the existing pinned USGS input.

The golden overview, yard view and low-Moon view each have a 640 by 360
preview at four samples per pixel and a 720p input at 64 samples per pixel.
Golden and lunar 4K inputs are also prepared, at 64 samples per pixel.
Use a measured smaller run before scheduling either 4K input.

Three prescribed layers give the mist different depths and patch sizes.
Wind advances their three-dimensional noise at the requested frame time.
This produces authored weather structure; vertical circulation, rain,
hail and lightning remain on the feature backlog.

The night camera looks across the forest toward a full Moon above the
observatory. Its textured lunar face comes from the existing shelf. The
directions and exposure are authored, not a dated ephemeris.

The night inputs deliberately omit a Sun block. A Sun below the horizon
still adds twilight skylight; raising the exposure to reveal moonlit mist
also raises that separate illumination.

The Moon already uses the renderer's full-Moon spectrum without a Sun
block. Dusk studies can retain solar twilight, but that is a second light
source, not just a phase setting.

Surfaces currently receive sky illumination while mist scattering uses
explicit lamps. That transport limitation remains open in Light-1.

The independent direction checks and the night-input guard cover the scene
mistake; they do not claim to repair that separate limitation. Fermat
replays the same CPU calculation and checks reproducibility.

Run through Game 5's GPU lane, using the reviewed renderer build.
Keep each actual PNG with its scene, source catalogue, producer identity,
timings and observed memory record under `scratch/renders`. Inspect the
preview before choosing a larger view, then update the hero-stills HTML.
