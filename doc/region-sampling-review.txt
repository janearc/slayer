Region sampling review

Five 32 by 32 patches in the recorded 2048 by 1820 street study have raw
linear-light comparisons. Each uses the same recorded executable and set,
software GPU, fixed exposure and fresh draws. The reports name the hashes,
coordinates, thresholds, scores and limitations. Existing scene caches were
used; complete transitive input closure has not been established.

Mean absolute RGB difference against the 32-per-side reference:
                1/side     2/side     4/side     16/side
Water           0.580653   0.762536   0.841461   0.630330
Wall            0.013001   0.007951   0.004234   0.001751
Haze            0.050309   0.028586   0.017689   0.007339
Lamps           0.099712   0.051567   0.025610   0.007638
End             0.861611   0.461575   0.256102   0.079692

Water does not show a monotonic mean difference in this sequence; the other
four do. Isolated-positive counts do not all follow the same pattern and use
patch-specific thresholds; consult the individual reports rather than compare
counts across patches. These are observations of particular finite draws, not
a universal sampling rule or an identified cause of bright pixels.

The water patch also has a 64-per-side reference: its 32-per-side frame has
mean absolute RGB difference 0.500921 against that finer draw. A single 64
reference is also finite. No reference has been shown converged and raw draw
repeat variation was not measured in these studies. For water, delivered
pixels from one prior repeat matched for each sample setting; that is not a
raw repeat measurement. The nearly white end sheet hides much of its raw
variation. Review the images as well as the numbers.

Reports: region-water-linear-report.txt, region-water-64-report.txt,
region-wall-linear-report.txt, region-haze-linear-report.txt,
region-lamps-linear-report.txt and region-end-linear-report.txt. Each gallery
folder holds its inspected sheet, source raw frames, score JSON and helper.
