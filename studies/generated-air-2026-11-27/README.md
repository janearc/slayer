clouds, same seed and light, camera, 800 × 800, 2 rays/axis and exposure
0.003 within each view. All three authored shells use 8.5 km scale height
and linear RGB [0.5, 0.7, 1]. Only the surface loss changes: 0.012,

8.5 km value is not asserted to equal the previous derived scale height.

muted ground and a narrower limb, while the thin setting shows green/brown
ground and a subdued rim. In the orbital view the thinner settings reveal
more of the underlying surface, but the thinnest globe is very dark at
the fixed exposure. The near ground remains fuzzy and has no recognizable
mountain relief. The comparison demonstrates a visible art control, not a
validation of atmospheric physics or terrain quality. Reproduced PNG and raw PFM files go to
