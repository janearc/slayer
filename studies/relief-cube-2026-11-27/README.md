# Cube-sphere relief comparison

Jobs 295–296 used experimental binary 305399382 with a level-six uniform
cube-sphere mesh (49,152 faces). The field, fixed angular filter, 10 km
height scale and cameras match the earlier 256 × 128 latitude-longitude
mesh controls, although the two representations do not have equal face
counts. Both values and the target's thin air are authored artistic choices.

At these 800 × 800 views the cube images look similar to the corresponding
latitude-longitude controls. The orbital image has faint land gridding; no
obvious cube-face seam was seen, but neither view shows recognizable
mountains, silhouette relief or clear cast shadows. These pictures do not
establish equivalence at other locations or seam/pole correctness. This is
an experiment, not a finished or accepted adaptive ground renderer. The saved jobs write
PNGs and PFM raws to `out/studies/2026-11-27-relief-cube-{orbit,target}/`.
