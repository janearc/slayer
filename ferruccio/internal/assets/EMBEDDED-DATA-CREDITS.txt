Hānauma Bay elevation and water
The embedded elevation grid in scene/hanauma/noaa-cudem-window.bin.gz
uses a five-metre resampling of NOAA National Centers for Environmental
Information's Hawaiʻi 1/9 arc-second CUDEM tile
ncei19_n21x50_w157x75_2021v1.tif.
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/metadata/landing-page/bin/iso?id=gov.noaa.ngdc.mgg.dem%3A299919
https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/dem/NCEI_ninth_Topobathy_Hawaii_9428/tiles/ncei19_n21x50_w157x75_2021v1.tif

The embedded substrate grid in scene/hanauma/noaa-structure.bin.gz
rasterizes the Structure - 2007 polygons from the NOAA/NOS/NCCOS/MSE
Biogeography Branch. Slayer uses the result as broad substrate weights.
https://gis.ngdc.noaa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/nccos/BenthicMapping_Hawaii_Dynamic/MapServer/10
The derivation and checksums are recorded in scene/hanauma/README.md.

Generated giant planet radii
The numeric grid in scene/generate/fortney2007.txt transcribes giant
planet radii from Tables 2–4 of Fortney, J. J., Marley, M. S., and Barnes,
J. W. (2007), The Astrophysical Journal 659, 1661–1672.
https://doi.org/10.1086/512120
https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0612671v4
